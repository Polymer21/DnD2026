Making Your Character

Pick a role that sounds fun. We’ll handle the math. By the end of this page you’ll have a character you can bring to your first table.

1. Pick what sounds fun

Do you want to swing a sword, cast spells, sneak, or talk your way through trouble? Choose the feeling first. The class will follow.

2. Choose a background

A background is who you were before the adventure: a baker, a sailor, a scholar. It gives you a few skills and a story hook.

3. Let the sheet do the math

Your character sheet tracks the numbers. Your DM and table will help you fill it in at session zero.
