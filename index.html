01 — The notice board

Why first games go wrong

Pinned by the hearth

Problem 01

Thrown in the deep end

Rulebooks, jargon and a table of veterans before you know what a d20 is.

The fix

Bite-size guides and a first session built for people who have never played.

Problem 02

The wrong group

Mismatched expectations: you wanted story, they wanted a three-hour fight.

The fix

We match you on playstyle, schedule and goals before you sit down.

Problem 03

Cost or pressure

Paid tables, pricey books and the fear of slowing everyone down.

The fix

Free to start, beginners only, and a table code that makes questions welcome.
